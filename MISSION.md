# Mission

## Atlas Specifications Mission

The mission of Atlas Specifications is to define, maintain, and evolve the canonical normative specifications that establish the common language and behavioral requirements of the Atlas Knowledge Operating System.

Atlas Specifications provides a stable, implementation-independent foundation for knowledge representation, evidence management, reasoning, validation, discovery, metadata, and interoperability.

These specifications serve as the single source of truth for every Atlas standard, reference implementation, SDK, and application.

---

# Objectives

Atlas Specifications exists to:

- Define canonical concepts.
- Standardize knowledge representation.
- Enable independent implementations.
- Promote interoperability.
- Ensure long-term compatibility.
- Separate specifications from implementations.
- Support automated conformance testing.

---

# Responsibilities

Atlas Specifications is responsible for:

- Publishing normative specifications.
- Maintaining specification quality.
- Defining common terminology.
- Managing specification lifecycle.
- Governing specification evolution.
- Supporting standards development.

---

# Non-Goals

Atlas Specifications does **not**:

- Develop production software.
- Implement APIs.
- Provide SDKs.
- Define database schemas.
- Build applications.
- Replace Atlas Standards.

These responsibilities belong to other repositories.

---

# Principles

Atlas Specifications follows these principles:

- Specifications before implementations.
- Stability before expansion.
- Clarity before complexity.
- Compatibility before convenience.
- Traceability before optimization.
- Interoperability by design.
- Technology neutrality.

---

# Success Criteria

Atlas Specifications succeeds when:

- Every Atlas implementation follows the published specifications.
- Independent implementations remain interoperable.
- Specifications evolve without unnecessary breaking changes.
- Conformance can be objectively evaluated.
- Specifications remain understandable and maintainable over time.

---

# Guiding Statement

> Atlas Specifications defines what Atlas is.

> Atlas implementations define how Atlas works.
