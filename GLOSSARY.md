# Glossary

## Overview

This glossary defines the canonical terminology used throughout the Atlas Specifications.

Unless explicitly overridden by a normative specification, the definitions in this glossary apply to the entire Atlas ecosystem.

---

# A

## Approval

The governance decision that authorizes a specification for publication.

---

## Architecture

The fundamental organization of Atlas, including its components, relationships, and governing principles.

---

# C

## Canonical

The officially recognized representation within Atlas.

---

## Canonical Identifier

A permanent identifier assigned to an Atlas Object.

Canonical Identifiers MUST remain immutable.

---

## Claim

A statement or assertion that may be evaluated through evidence and validation.

Defined by AKS.

---

## Compatibility

The ability of different versions or implementations to operate together without violating normative requirements.

---

## Conformance

The degree to which an implementation satisfies the mandatory requirements of a specification.

---

# D

## Dataset

A structured collection of observations or evidence.

Defined by AKS.

---

## Discovery

Validated knowledge that contributes new understanding.

Defined by AKS.

---

## Draft

The initial lifecycle stage of a specification.

---

# E

## Entity

An identifiable object, concept, event, organization, or phenomenon represented within Atlas.

Defined by AKS.

---

## Evidence

Information that supports or contradicts one or more claims.

Defined by AES.

---

## Extension

An optional capability that augments a specification without violating mandatory requirements.

---

# G

## Governance

The process by which specifications evolve through review, approval, and publication.

---

# I

## Identifier

A unique identifier assigned to an Atlas Object.

Defined by ACCS.

---

## Informative

Content intended to explain or illustrate.

Informative content does not introduce implementation requirements.

---

## Interoperability

The ability of independent implementations to exchange and interpret information consistently.

Defined by AIS.

---

# K

## Knowledge

Validated understanding derived through observation, evidence, reasoning, and validation.

Defined by AKS.

---

# L

## Lifecycle

The sequence of maturity states through which a specification progresses.

---

# M

## Metadata

Information describing an object without changing its semantic meaning.

Defined by ACCS.

---

# N

## Namespace

The scope within which identifiers remain unique.

Defined by ACCS.

---

## Normative

Content that defines mandatory or optional implementation requirements.

Normative content is evaluated during conformance testing.

---

# O

## Object

The fundamental representational unit within Atlas.

Defined by ACCS.

---

## Observation

A representation of something directly observed.

Defined by AKS.

---

# P

## Property

A named characteristic of an Object.

Defined by ACCS.

---

# Q

## Question

An inquiry that may lead to new knowledge or discovery.

Defined by AKS.

---

# R

## Reasoning

The process of deriving new knowledge from existing validated knowledge.

Defined by ARS.

---

## Reference

A relationship pointing to another Atlas Object or external resource.

Defined by ACCS.

---

## Repository

A version-controlled collection of Atlas specifications or implementations.

---

## Requirement

A normative statement against which implementations may be evaluated.

---

# S

## Source

The origin from which evidence is obtained.

Defined by AES.

---

## Specification

A normative document defining implementation-independent requirements.

---

## Stable

A lifecycle state indicating that a specification is recommended for production implementations.

---

## Status

The lifecycle condition of a specification or object.

Defined by ACCS.

---

# T

## Traceability

The ability to determine the origin, evolution, and relationships of knowledge and evidence.

---

# U

## URI

A globally addressable identifier or reference.

Defined by ACCS.

---

# V

## Validation

The process of evaluating whether claims are sufficiently supported by evidence.

Defined by AVS.

---

## Version

A revision identifier representing a specific state of a specification or object.

Defined by ACCS.

---
